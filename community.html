The Digital Life Challenge

Each round has one theme, for example “secure a parent’s phone” or “cancel three subscriptions”. Complete it, tell us what you did and what you learned, and you are entered.

How it works

  1. Register your interest with the form.
  2. You receive the current theme, closing date and the prizes on offer for that round.
  3. Submit a short write-up. Tips that help other readers score highest.
  4. Winners are contacted directly and, with permission, featured on the site.

Rules in brief

  • Free to enter. No purchase or donation is required and neither improves your chances.
  • One entry per person per round. Entrants must be 18 or older.
  • Prizes vary by round and are funded by supporters and prize partners. Each round’s prizes are confirmed before entries open.
  • Void where prohibited. Full terms accompany each round.
