The 7-day Digital Life Reset

Tick tasks off as you go. Progress is saved in this browser, and the page prints cleanly.

  • Day 1. Install a password manager and create one long master passphrase. Guide
  • Day 1. Change your main email password to a new unique one, stored in the manager.
  • Day 2. Turn on two-step login for email, banking, your phone account, cloud storage and your main social profile. Guide
  • Day 2. Save the backup codes somewhere offline.
  • Day 3. Check your email addresses for breaches and replace every exposed or reused password. Guide
  • Day 4. Switch on automatic backup for your phone and computer, and confirm one file restores. Guide
  • Day 4. Turn on automatic updates on every device, including the router. Guide
  • Day 5. Run the 30-minute privacy checkup: app permissions, ad settings, location history. Guide
  • Day 6. List every subscription and cancel at least one. Tool
  • Day 6. Enable transaction alerts on each card and bank account.
  • Day 7. Cut notifications to people and essentials, and set a sleep schedule. Guide
  • Day 7. Write a one-page legacy note and add legacy contacts. Tool
Finished? Re-take the Digital Life Score to see the difference, then share the plan with one person who needs it.
