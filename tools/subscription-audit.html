Subscription audit

Replace the examples with your own subscriptions. Totals update as you type and your list is saved in this browser only.

How to find every subscription

  1. Search your email for “receipt”, “renewal”, “subscription” and “trial”.
  2. Open the subscriptions list in your phone’s app store account.
  3. Scan three months of card and bank statements for repeating amounts.
  4. Check payment services and any old cards still on file.

Reading the numbers

The ten-year figure is simply the monthly total multiplied by 120, with no price rises assumed, so reality is usually higher. The invested figure assumes the same monthly amount earning a 7% average annual return for ten years. It is an illustration of opportunity cost, not a forecast or financial advice.

The keep, pause, cancel test

For each line ask: did I use this in the last 30 days, and would I sign up again today at this price? Two yeses means keep. One means pause or downgrade. None means cancel now; you can always return.
