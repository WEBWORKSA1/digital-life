Screen time calculator

Daily hours feel small. Seen across a year, or a life, they are one of the biggest decisions you make. Use the figure from your phone’s screen time or digital wellbeing settings.

How the numbers are worked out

Days per year is your daily hours multiplied by 365 and divided by 24. The share of waking hours assumes 16 hours awake. Years by age 80 multiplies your daily share by the years remaining. These are simple arithmetic, not predictions about your health or lifespan.

It is not all wasted time

Screen time includes calls with family, maps, reading and learning. The goal is not zero. The useful question is which hours you would choose again and which simply happened to you. Most phones can show time per app, which usually reveals one or two apps taking the bulk of it.

Three changes with the best return

  • Turn off every notification that is not from a person or genuinely time-critical.
  • Move the most distracting apps off the first home screen and set a daily limit.
  • Charge the phone outside the bedroom and use an ordinary alarm clock.
