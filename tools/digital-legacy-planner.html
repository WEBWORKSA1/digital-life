Digital legacy planner

If you were unreachable tomorrow, would anyone know which accounts exist, which matter and what you want done with them? List them here, then print the page and store it with your important papers.

What to include

  • Email accounts, because they unlock everything else.
  • Money: banking, investments, payment apps, loyalty points and any cryptocurrency.
  • Memories: photo libraries, cloud drives and messaging backups.
  • Identity: social profiles, domains, websites and creative work.
  • Recurring charges that should be cancelled.
  • Devices and how they are unlocked.

Keep secrets out of the list

This planner records what exists and where the instructions are, not the passwords themselves. Safer options for the secrets are your password manager’s emergency access feature, or a sealed envelope held with your will by someone you trust.

Then use the built-in tools

Major email, phone and social platforms let you name a legacy or inactive-account contact in their settings. It takes a few minutes per service and avoids a slow, document-heavy process for your family later.
