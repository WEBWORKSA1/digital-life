How the score works

Each answer earns zero, one or two points. Three questions cover each pillar, so every pillar is scored out of six and the total out of thirty, shown as a percentage.

  • 85 to 100, Thriving. The fundamentals are in place. Re-check twice a year.
  • 65 to 84, Steady. Solid, with a few gaps an attacker or a hardware failure could find.
  • 40 to 64, Exposed. One reused password or lost phone away from a bad week.
  • Below 40, At risk. Start with email security and backups today.

The questions reflect the basics that official bodies agree on: unique passwords in a manager, multifactor login, automatic updates, backups and scepticism towards unexpected messages. We added money, wellbeing and legacy because those are where people tell us they feel least in control.

The score is a self-assessment for education, not a security audit.
