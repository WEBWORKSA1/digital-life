How to read a breach entry

The date is when it happened

Breaches are frequently published long after the event. An old date does not mean an old risk if you never changed that password.

Exposed data decides your response

Email only means more phishing. Passwords mean change them now. Identity numbers or financial data mean alerts and credit monitoring.

Reuse is the real danger

Attackers try leaked passwords on other sites within hours. A unique password per account limits a breach to one account.
