About

Why Digital.Life exists

Nobody was taught how to run a digital life. We picked it up one account, one device and one mistake at a time.

The result is familiar: hundreds of logins, a handful of reused passwords, photos scattered across services, subscriptions nobody remembers starting, and a phone that interrupts more than it helps. Advice exists, but it is split between security experts, privacy advocates, finance writers and wellbeing researchers who rarely talk to each other.

Digital.Life brings those threads together for ordinary people. We organise everything around five pillars: security, privacy, money, wellbeing and legacy. Each has tools you can use immediately and guides that explain the reasoning.

How we work

  • Action first. Every guide ends in something you can do today.
  • Private by design. Our tools run in your browser. What you type into them is not sent to us.
  • Sources you can check. We lean on official guidance from bodies such as CISA, the FTC and the UK NCSC, and link to it on our resources page.
  • No fear selling. We describe risk in proportion and do not exaggerate to sell products.
  • Corrections welcome. Apps change quickly. If something is out of date, tell us and we will fix it.

How we are funded

The site is free to use. It is funded by advertising served through Google AdSense, by reader donations and by clearly labelled sponsorships. Advertisers and sponsors have no say over what we publish or recommend.
