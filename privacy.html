Privacy policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

This policy explains what information Digital.Life (“we”, “the site”) handles when you visit, and the choices you have.

Information you give us

When you submit a form (newsletter and reset plan sign-up, contact, donation pledge, contest registration or contributor application) we receive the details you enter, such as your name, email address and message. We use them only to respond, to send the updates you asked for, to administer contests and to process support you offer. Form submissions are delivered to us by a third-party form delivery service (FormSubmit), which processes the data on our behalf. We do not sell your information.

Information our tools handle

The interactive tools (score, password checker, passphrase generator, scam checker, calculators, checklists and planners) run entirely in your browser. What you type into them is not transmitted to us. Some tools save your progress in your browser’s local storage on your own device; you can clear it at any time through your browser settings.

Cookies and advertising

We use Google AdSense to show advertising. Google and its partners, as third-party vendors, use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and other sites on the internet.

  • You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings.
  • You can opt out of some third-party vendors’ use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info.
  • Learn how Google uses data from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland may be shown a consent message before personalised ads are served.

Other third-party services

To keep pages current, your browser requests data directly from third parties: Have I Been Pwned (breach list), an RSS conversion service (news and video listings), YouTube (video thumbnails and, when you press play, the privacy-enhanced player) and Google Fonts (typefaces). These services receive standard technical information such as your IP address and are governed by their own privacy policies. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log visits for security purposes.

Children

The site is written for adults and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information we hold about you, or to object to its use. Send a request through the contact page and we will respond within a reasonable time.

Data retention

We keep form submissions only as long as needed for the purpose they were sent for, and delete them on request.

Changes

We may update this policy as the site evolves. The date at the top shows the latest revision.

Contact

Privacy questions can be sent through the contact page.
